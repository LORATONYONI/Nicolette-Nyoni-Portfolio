About

I build data pipelines that teams can trust.

I design and run the pipelines behind production analytics and AI: ingesting from SFTP, Oracle, Kafka, and SaaS sources, landing data in Hive, Iceberg, and AWS, and proving it is correct with reconciliation and checksum checks. I came to engineering through analytics, so I build with the people downstream in mind: the analyst, the dashboard, or the fraud model that needs the data on time and right.

What you can expect

  • Idempotent, duplicate-safe loads that are safe to rerun
  • Validation, reconciliation, and checksums built into every pipeline
  • Clear documentation: source-to-target mappings, backfill plans, runbooks
  • Plain-language updates for stakeholders and leadership
